# Third-Party Material

ASM3D contains no third-party code. The only third-party material is font data.

## DejaVu fonts

`engine/ui/a3_font_data.c` contains glyph bitmaps that
`tools/gen_font_atlas.py` rasterized from **DejaVu Sans**, **DejaVu Sans Bold**
and **DejaVu Sans Mono**.

DejaVu fonts are based on Bitstream Vera. They are distributed under the
